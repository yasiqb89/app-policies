Privacy Policy

Seven · Effective 5 November 2026

Seven is a to-do app for one week, Monday to Sunday. Without an account, what you write stays on your device. If you choose to sign in, your week is also kept on your account so it can sync between your devices.

  • You can use Seven without an account. Nothing you write leaves your device unless you sign in or send it somewhere yourself.
  • Signing in with Apple or Google is optional. It stores your email address, and your name if Apple or Google provides it, with your lines and settings, so one week can sync across your devices.
  • Pictures, recordings, drawings and files are never synced. They stay on the device they were made on.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools are built in.
  • Your information is not sold, and it is not shared except with the service that runs the account for us.
  • You can delete your account, and what it holds, inside the app.

This policy applies to the Seven app on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android (“Seven”, “the app”), published by Yassir (“we”, “us”). It explains what the app handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

If you do not sign in, we collect no personal information. Seven does not send your tasks, notes, recordings, pictures, calendar items, voice or usage to us or to any server we operate.

If you sign in, the following is stored on your account:

  • Sign-in details: your email address (or the private relay address, if you use Apple’s Hide My Email), your name if Apple or Google provides it, and an account ID.
  • What you write: your lines and their days, times, reminders, notes and steps; your Anytime list, day notes, goals and repeating lines; and whether each is done, moved or let go. This includes lines you brought in from Reminders or Calendar.
  • Your settings: the ones that follow you between devices, such as what a good day holds and your task mark. Settings that belong to one device, such as its look, sound and voice, are not sent.
  • Your devices: a random ID for each signed-in device, its type, its name (for example the name you gave your iPhone) and when it last synced, so you can see and sign out your devices in the app.

We use this only to sign you in and to keep your week in step across your devices. We do not read your content for any other purpose.

We also receive anything you send us yourself, for example by email (see Contact).

2. What is stored on your device

To work, Seven keeps the following in its own private storage on your device:

  • The lines (tasks) you write, their days, times, steps, notes and reminders.
  • Things you choose to keep with a line: photos, files, voice notes and drawings.
  • Your closed weeks (the Ledger), habits Seven has noticed from your own finished lines, and your settings.
  • A small summary of the current week for the home-screen and lock-screen widgets, kept in storage shared only between Seven and its own widgets and shortcuts on the same device.

Without an account, this information stays on the device. If you sign in, the items listed in section 1 also go to your account; photos, files, voice notes and drawings do not. Seven keeps one local copy of your data on the device just before its first upload, in case anything goes wrong. Your device’s own backup (for example iCloud Backup or Google’s device backup) may include this information if you have backups switched on; those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their terms, not by us. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on that device.

3. Permissions and how each one is used

Seven asks for a permission only at the moment you use the feature that needs it. You can refuse, and you can change your answer at any time in your device’s Settings. The rest of the app keeps working.

Reminders and Calendar (Bring in)

If you turn on Bring in, Seven reads your reminders (iPhone) and calendar events (iPhone and Android) on the device so that things due this week appear on their day. You choose which lists and calendars are read. Meetings stay in your calendar unless you choose to bring them in. Seven never writes to your calendar. On iPhone, ticking a line that came from Reminders also marks it complete in Reminders; nothing else is written back. Seven reads this on the device. If you are signed in, a reminder or event you bring in becomes a line and syncs with your other lines; what is not brought in is never sent.

Microphone and speech recognition

You can speak instead of typing, and record voice notes. Speech is turned into text by your phone’s own speech recognition. By default (Settings › Voice › Only on this phone), Seven only uses recognition that runs on the device, so what you say does not leave it. If your phone cannot recognise speech on the device and you choose to switch that setting off, your phone’s speech service (Apple’s or Google’s) may process the audio on their servers under Apple’s or Google’s privacy policy. Voice notes you record are saved only on your device. If you are signed in, the words of a line you speak sync like any other line.

On-device intelligence

Where your phone supports it, Seven can use the language model built into the phone (Apple Intelligence on supported iPhones, Gemini Nano on supported Android phones) to understand what you typed or said and to suggest a day. These models run on the device; Seven does not send your words to any online AI service.

Camera and photos

You can take a photo or pick one to keep with a line. Picking a photo uses the system picker, which gives Seven only the photo you choose; Seven does not read your photo library and never saves anything to it. Photos are stored only inside the app, on that device, and are not synced.

Notifications

Reminders and the optional evening and Sunday prompts are scheduled locally on your phone. They are not sent through any server. On Android, Seven asks to schedule exact alarms so a reminder rings at the minute you chose.

Sign in with Apple and Google

If you choose to sign in, Apple or Google confirms who you are and gives us the sign-in details listed in section 1. That step is handled by them under Apple’s or Google’s privacy policy. Your session is kept in your device’s secure storage.

Siri, Shortcuts, widgets and sharing into Seven

If you add a line through Siri, Shortcuts, the Action Button, Control Center, Quick Settings, a widget or the share sheet, the text is handed to Seven on your device. When you speak to Siri, Siri’s dictation is provided by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

4. Things you send yourself

Some features create something you can send elsewhere. They happen only when you tap them, and the information goes only where you choose:

  • Sharing or copying a line as words or a picture.
  • Exporting your data (text, JSON or CSV) or saving a full copy of Seven to a place you pick, such as Files or Google Drive.
  • Copying a diagnostic summary to your clipboard to send to us if you ask for help.

Once you send these, the service you sent them to handles them under its own terms.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not use it for advertising. Seven contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

The account runs on Supabase, which stores it for us as our service provider. Supabase’s servers may be in a different country from you. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

6. Data retention and deletion

On your device, your information stays for as long as you keep it there. You can:

  • Delete any line, closed line or habit in the app.
  • Erase everything on a device with Settings › Your data › Start over. This does not change your account.
  • Delete the app, which removes everything it stored on that device.

On your account, your information stays until you delete the account. A line you delete leaves your devices, but its record stays on the account, marked as deleted, so that it cannot return on another device. Signing out stops syncing on that device; it does not delete your account.

To delete your account: in Seven, open Settings › Account and choose Delete account. Your sign-in and everything the account holds are deleted at once and cannot be recovered. Each device keeps its own copy of your week until you remove it. If you can no longer open the app, email us from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.

If you email us, we keep that conversation only as long as needed to help you, and you can ask us to delete it.

7. Security

On your device, your information is protected by the device’s own security: the app’s storage is private to Seven and covered by your passcode and encryption. On your account, information travels over encrypted connections, and access rules on the server keep each person’s rows to that person alone. No system is perfectly secure; keeping your device locked and up to date is the best protection for what you keep in Seven.

8. Children

Seven is a general productivity app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete or take a copy of personal information held about you, and to complain to your data protection authority. Without an account, we hold none. With an account, we process your information to provide the sync you asked for. You can correct your lines in the app, export them from Settings › Your data, and delete your account as described in section 6. For anything else, write to us.

10. Changes to this policy

If Seven changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the app.
