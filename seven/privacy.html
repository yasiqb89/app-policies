Privacy Policy

Seven · Effective 13 October 2026

Seven is a to-do app for one week, Monday to Sunday. It is built so that what you write stays on your phone. Seven has no account, no server and no analytics, and the developer does not collect, receive or sell any of your information.

  • Everything you put in Seven is stored only on your device.
  • There is no sign-in, no account and no cloud sync run by us.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools are built in.
  • Reminders, Calendar, microphone, camera and photos are used only when you turn on the feature that needs them, and only on your device.
  • Nothing is shared with or sold to anyone.

This policy applies to the Seven app on iPhone, iPad and Android (“Seven”, “the app”), published by Yassir (“we”, “us”). It explains what the app handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Seven does not send your tasks, notes, recordings, pictures, calendar items, voice or usage to us or to any server we operate. We have no way of seeing what you keep in the app.

The only time we receive anything from you is if you contact us yourself, for example by email (see Contact).

2. What is stored on your device

To work, Seven keeps the following in its own private storage on your phone:

  • The lines (tasks) you write, their days, times, steps, notes and reminders.
  • Things you choose to keep with a line: photos, files, voice notes and drawings.
  • Your closed weeks (the Ledger), habits Seven has noticed from your own finished lines, and your settings.
  • A small summary of the current week for the home-screen and lock-screen widgets, kept in storage shared only between Seven and its own widgets and shortcuts on the same device.

This information stays on the device. Your phone’s own backup (for example iCloud Backup or Google’s device backup) may include it if you have backups switched on; those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their terms, not by us. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

3. Permissions and how each one is used

Seven asks for a permission only at the moment you use the feature that needs it. You can refuse, and you can change your answer at any time in your phone’s Settings. The rest of the app keeps working.

Reminders and Calendar (Bring in)

If you turn on Bring in, Seven reads your reminders (iPhone) and calendar events (iPhone and Android) on the device so that things due this week appear on their day. You choose which lists and calendars are read. Meetings stay in your calendar unless you choose to bring them in. Seven never writes to your calendar. On iPhone, ticking a line that came from Reminders also marks it complete in Reminders; nothing else is written back. None of this information leaves your device.

Microphone and speech recognition

You can speak instead of typing, and record voice notes. Speech is turned into text by your phone’s own speech recognition. By default (Settings › Speaking › Only on this phone), Seven only uses recognition that runs on the device, so what you say does not leave it. If your phone cannot recognise speech on the device and you choose to switch that setting off, your phone’s speech service (Apple’s or Google’s) may process the audio on their servers under Apple’s or Google’s privacy policy. Voice notes you record are saved only on your device.

On-device intelligence

Where your phone supports it, Seven can use the language model built into the phone (Apple Intelligence on supported iPhones, Gemini Nano on supported Android phones) to understand what you typed or said and to suggest a day. These models run on the device; Seven does not send your words to any online AI service.

Camera and photos

You can take a photo or pick one to keep with a line. Picking a photo uses the system picker, which gives Seven only the photo you choose; Seven does not read your photo library and never saves anything to it. Photos are stored only inside the app.

Notifications

Reminders and the optional evening and Sunday prompts are scheduled locally on your phone. They are not sent through any server. On Android, Seven asks to schedule exact alarms so a reminder rings at the minute you chose.

Siri, Shortcuts, widgets and sharing into Seven

If you add a line through Siri, Shortcuts, the Action Button, Control Center, Quick Settings, a widget or the share sheet, the text is handed to Seven on your device. When you speak to Siri, Siri’s dictation is provided by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

4. Things that leave your phone only when you send them

Some features create something you can send elsewhere. They happen only when you tap them, and the information goes only where you choose:

  • Sharing or copying a line as words or a picture.
  • Exporting your data (text, JSON or CSV) or saving a full copy of Seven to a place you pick, such as Files or Google Drive.
  • Copying a diagnostic summary to your clipboard to send to us if you ask for help.

Once you send these, the service you sent them to handles them under its own terms.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not share, sell, rent or trade any information, because we do not have any. Seven contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your information stays on your device for as long as you keep it there. You can:

  • Delete any line, closed line or habit in the app.
  • Erase everything with Settings › On this phone › Start over.
  • Delete the app, which removes everything it stored.

Because there is no account, there is no account to delete and nothing held by us to request. If you email us, we keep that conversation only as long as needed to help you, and you can ask us to delete it.

7. Security

Your information is protected by your phone’s own security: the app’s storage is private to Seven and covered by your device’s passcode and encryption. Keeping your device locked and up to date is the best protection for what you keep in Seven.

8. Children

Seven is a general productivity app and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA give you rights to access, correct or delete personal information a company holds about you. Since we hold none, all of your Seven data is already in your hands on your device. If you have contacted us by email and want a copy or deletion of that correspondence, write to us.

10. Changes to this policy

If Seven changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the app.
