It helps to include your phone model, its system version and the version of Seven (at the foot of Seven’s Settings). You can also copy a diagnostic summary from Settings › On this phone and paste it into your email. It contains no tasks or personal content.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. Seven has no account and no server. See the privacy policy.

How do I move Seven to a new phone?

In Settings › On this phone, save a copy of Seven to Files or Google Drive, then on the new phone choose Restore from that copy. Your phone’s own backup may also carry Seven across.

Bring in shows nothing from Reminders or Calendar.

Check that Seven is allowed to read them in your phone’s Settings, and that the lists or calendars you want are ticked in Seven’s Settings › Bring in.

The microphone is missing.

Seven listens only on the device by default. If your phone cannot recognise your language on the device, the mic is hidden. You can change this in Settings › Speaking › Only on this phone.

How do I delete everything?

Settings › On this phone › Start over, or delete the app.
