Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device. If you sign in with Apple or Google, your lines and settings are also kept on your account so they sync between your devices. Pictures, recordings and drawings stay on the device they were made on. See the privacy policy.

How do I move Seven to a new phone?

Sign in on the new phone with the same account and your week comes across. Pictures and recordings do not. To move everything, in Settings › Your data, save a copy of Seven to Files or Google Drive, then on the new phone choose Restore from that copy. Your phone’s own backup may also carry Seven across.

Bring in shows nothing from Reminders or Calendar.

Check that Seven is allowed to read them in your phone’s Settings, and that the lists or calendars you want are ticked in Seven’s Settings › Bring in.

The microphone is missing.

Seven listens only on the device by default. If your phone cannot recognise your language on the device, the mic is hidden. You can change this in Settings › Voice › Only on this phone.

How do I delete everything?

On a device: Settings › Your data › Start over, or delete the app. On your account: Settings › Account › Delete account.
